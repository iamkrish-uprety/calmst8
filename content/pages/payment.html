We want paying at CALMST8 to be safe and straightforward.

Payment methods

The payment methods available to you are shown at checkout. Prices are in pounds sterling (GBP).

Security

Payments are processed securely at checkout by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card details.

When you are charged

Your payment is taken when you place your order. If we need to cancel an order, for example because an item is out of stock, you will be refunded in full to your original payment method.

Problems with payment

If something goes wrong at checkout or you are charged unexpectedly, email help@calmst8.com with your order number and we will look into it.
