When will I get my order?

We take 1-3 business days to process your order, then delivery takes 3-7 business days. That is 4-10 business days in total in most cases.

How much does shipping cost?

UK delivery is a flat £2.99 per order.

Do you ship outside the UK?

Not at the moment. We currently ship within the UK only.

Will I get a shipping confirmation?

Yes. You will receive an email as soon as your order is on its way.

What is your returns policy?

You have 14 days from delivery to request a return. Items must be unused and in their original condition, and return postage is paid by the customer. See our Returns and Refund Policy.

How do I start a return?

Email help@calmst8.com with your order number within 14 days of delivery and we will send the return instructions.

What if my item is faulty or incorrect?

Email us with your order number and photos and we will put it right.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment is taken securely at checkout, and the options available to you are shown there. See our Payment Policy.

How can I contact you?

Email help@calmst8.com or message us on Instagram @calmst8.
