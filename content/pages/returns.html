If your CALMST8 purchase is not quite right, our returns process is simple.

Returns window

You have 14 days from the day you receive your order to request a return. To be eligible, the item must be unused, unwashed, in its original condition and with any tags attached.

How to start a return

  1. Email help@calmst8.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and the item(s) you want to return.
  2. We will reply with the return instructions.
  3. Send the item(s) back to us. We recommend using a tracked service, as we cannot be responsible for returns that do not reach us.

Return postage

The customer is responsible for the cost of return postage, unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong item.

Different size

If you would like a different size, email us and we will help where stock allows.

Refunds

Once we have received and inspected your return, we will refund the price of the returned item(s) to your original payment method. Your bank or card provider may need a few extra days to show the refund. Original delivery charges are not refunded unless the item was faulty or incorrect.

Faulty or incorrect items

If your order arrives faulty, damaged or not as described, email us at help@calmst8.com with your order number and photos of the problem and we will put it right. This policy does not affect your statutory rights.

Questions?

Email help@calmst8.com or message us on Instagram @calmst8.
