Shipping policy

At CALMST8 we want getting your order to be simple and transparent: one flat delivery price, clear timings and no hidden extras.

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses within the United Kingdom only.

Shipping costs

Delivery within the UK is a flat rate of £2.99 per order. The price is shown at checkout before you pay, so there are no surprises.

Shipping times

  • Processing time: 1-3 business days before dispatch.
  • Delivery time: 3-7 business days from dispatch.
  • Total estimated time: 4-10 business days in most cases.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.

Order confirmation

When your order is on its way you will receive an email confirming that it has shipped.

Possible delays

We always aim to meet these timings, but delays can occasionally happen because of high demand, weather or issues with delivery partners. If your order is delayed we will keep you informed.

Incorrect address

Please check your delivery address before you complete your order. If you notice a mistake, email us straight away at help@calmst8.com with your order number and we will do our best to update it before dispatch. We cannot be responsible for orders delivered to an incorrect address entered at checkout.

Need help?

If you have any questions about your delivery, email us at help@calmst8.com or message us on Instagram @calmst8.
